\section{Executive Summary \& Business Alignment}

\subsection{Business Context \& Legacy Bottlenecks}
TechViet Innovations (TVI), a rapidly expanding technology and logistics conglomerate in Vietnam, operates across three mission-critical campuses:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt]
    \item \textbf{Ho Chi Minh City Headquarters (HQ):} Houses corporate executive offices, finance systems, core engineering teams, and the primary enterprise Data Center supporting \textbf{2,000+ internal users}.
    \item \textbf{Hanoi R\&D and Engineering Campus:} Hosts artificial intelligence (AI) compute laboratories and software engineering divisions requiring high-throughput East--West connectivity for \textbf{1,500+ users}, alongside an isolated guest network for external partners.
    \item \textbf{Da Nang Logistics and Distribution Hub:} Operates automated warehouse telemetry, autonomous guided vehicles (AGVs), and real-time inventory IoT sensors supporting \textbf{500+ staff}.
\end{itemize}

TVI's legacy network relied on a flat Layer 2 topology and traditional hub-and-spoke WAN routing that backhauled all branch Internet traffic through Ho Chi Minh City. As cloud SaaS adoption, hybrid workforces, and AI/IoT telemetry expanded, this legacy architecture introduced four severe operational bottlenecks: (1) frequent Layer 2 broadcast storms causing enterprise-wide outages; (2) excessive WAN latency due to centralized Internet backhauling; (3) single points of failure at campus distribution gateways; and (4) coarse perimeter security unable to isolate untrusted Guest Wi-Fi or IoT devices from sensitive financial workloads.

\subsection{Translating Business Requirements into Technical Architecture}
To sustain an immediate base of \textbf{4,000+ internal users} and \textbf{10,000+ IoT endpoints}---while scaling cleanly beyond \textbf{10,000+ users by 2030} at \textbf{99.99\% (Four Nines)} availability---this project establishes a unified, dual-stack enterprise network engineered around five strategic pillars summarized in Table~\ref{tab:business_alignment}.
